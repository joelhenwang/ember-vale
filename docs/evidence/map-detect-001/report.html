Can an AI find the places on a painted map?

Yes. On five maps you painted, Gemini 3.8 Flash found all 50 places, named the right kind for 49, and dropped each pin a median of about 1% of the map's width from the drawing, with or without labels. It invented nothing: everything it listed beyond my list is really on the map. About 14 seconds and under one cent per map.

A place counts as found when the pin lands within 6% of the map's width of its drawing (15% for areas such as forests, mountains, rivers and roads). On labelled maps the name must match too. "Right kind" allows close words (a castle city for a city, a gatehouse for a checkpoint). Total for this test: $0.17.

What this means for the world graph

Pulling places out of a painted map is reliable enough to build on: every map style, labelled or not, came back complete and well placed. The player would confirm the pins (drag one if it sits a little off) rather than place them all by hand.
