Can an AI trace which places connect?

Yes, with the right model. Given the places on each of your five maps, Claude Sonnet 5.5 found 31 of the 32 clearly drawn connections and claimed no wrong ones: roads, mountain paths, a village track up to a cairn, and the dotted sea lanes between islands. About 20 seconds and 3 cents per map. Its traced points follow the drawn roads closely enough to compare route lengths.

The one miss

On the archipelago, the sea lane from Corvane to Saltmarket joins the Tidemark lane partway across. Every model followed Corvane's other lane to Hollow Isle (also really there) and missed the merge. Junctions where two routes meet are the hard case.

What this means for the world graph

Places (Gemini 3.8 Flash, test 1) plus connections (Sonnet 5.5, this test) give the whole graph from a painted map for about 4 cents and half a minute. The player confirms it, and the traced routes give each connection a length to turn into turns or days.
